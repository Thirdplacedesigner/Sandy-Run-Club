Step Into The Blue

Not a morning person?
Neither were we.

Let's be real—between homework, screens, and just needing sleep, finding the motivation to work out before school feels impossible. The pressure of strict competition or intimidating workouts can make physical activity feel like a chore rather than something to look forward to.

That’s why Sandy Run Club is different.

We meeting on Friday afternoons at 15:15 to completely bypass the early-morning alarm. There's no pressure to be "sporty" or competitive here. It doesn't matter if you run fast or prefer a relaxed walk while chatting with friends. This is your time to disconnect, get moving, and meet other students who are ready to upward push one another.

"The hardest step is out your front door."

The Friday Ritual

Every Friday at 15:15 PM. No scoreboards, no start lines—just a unified, welcoming move downward to the water and back.

01 /The Journey Out

We head out right after classes. A comfortable, 15-minute collective run from school straight down to Sandringham Beach to clear your mind and reset.

02 /The Beach Break

A 10-minute pause on the sand. Catch your breath, enjoy the coastal view, and if you are feeling adventurous, take an optional ocean dip to completely refresh.

03 /Return & Connect

A 15-minute run back to school. We gather together to share laughs, enjoy drinks or snacks, and walk into your first class feeling energized.
