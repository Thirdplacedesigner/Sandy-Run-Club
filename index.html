The Friday Blueprint

YOUR AFTERNOON ESCAPE

15:15 PM

The Departure

The final bell rings. We meet up on campus, leaving the stress in the lockers. We head out for a collective 15-minute run down to the coast. No stopwatches, just the sound of footsteps and conversation.

15:30 PM

The Coastal Reset

We hit the sand for a 10-minute pause. Catch your breath, look out at the ocean, and let the week wash away. Feeling bold? Dive in for an optional ocean dip to shock the system and awaken the senses.

15:40 PM

The Return

Energized and clear-headed, we take the 15-minute journey back to school. We arrive not as exhausted students, but as a unified crew ready to dominate the weekend.

15:55 PM

The Connection

We gather up back at basecamp. We share laughs, grab drinks, and celebrate the shared joy of moving our bodies. This is where friendships are forged.
