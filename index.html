The Friday Flow

Every Friday at 15:15. A seamless transition from the school week into the weekend. No pressure, just vibes.

01

The Journey Out

We head out right after classes. A comfortable, 15-minute collective run from school straight down to Sandringham Beach to clear your mind.

02

The Beach Break

A 10-minute pause on the sand. Catch your breath, enjoy the coastal view, and if you want, take an optional ocean dip to refresh.

03

Return & Connect

A 15-minute run back to school. We gather together to share laughs, grab drinks, and celebrate finishing the week strong.

Built By The Crew

We built this club based on real feedback from you. Here is why our setup works.

Goodbye Morning Alarms

By meeting on Friday afternoons, we completely skip the brutal 6:00 AM wake-ups and cold mornings that stop people from moving.

Based on survey feedback: "I want to stay in bed" & "cold weather"

Zero Intimidation

No stopwatches. No pressure. Whether you sprint or walk and talk, the focus is purely on shaking off the school week together.

Based on survey feedback: Worries about "how I will be looked at"

Real Mental Health

We run for clarity, not for medals. Combining coastal fresh air with a solid group of friends is the ultimate reset button.

Based on survey feedback: Wanting "improved health, strong mental state"
