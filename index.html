Start The Journey

Not a morning person?
Neither were we.

Let's be real—between homework, screens, and just needing sleep, finding the motivation to work out before school feels impossible. The pressure of being "sporty" or keeping up with highly competitive athletes is enough to keep anyone in bed.

That’s exactly why we built Sandy Run Club.

We dropped the competition, erased the pressure, and built a community. It doesn't matter if you run a five-minute mile or walk the whole way chatting with your mates. This is about stepping outside, getting moving, and starting your day with people who push you upward.

The Friday Ritual

Every Friday from 7:30 AM to 8:40 AM. No stress, no finish lines. Just a morning dedicated to good health, great vibes, and ticking off your physical activity goals without even thinking about it.

01. The Stride

We meet up and head out. A casual, 30-minute collective run from the campus straight down to the coastline as the sun comes up.

02. The Splash

Drop the shoes. We hit the beach for a quick, optional ocean dip to shock the system, wash away the sleep, and totally reset.

03. The Recharge

We roll into the local café. Grab a warm latte or a smoothie, hang out, and walk into your first class feeling completely unstoppable.

"Half your daily activity goals, crushed before 9:00 AM. Leave your weekend wide open."

Every morning you show up and run with us, you earn exclusive canteen tickets funded by our local events. Present them at recess or lunch, and treat yourself to free snacks and food on us.

Show up for the community. Stay for the vibes. Get rewarded for the effort.
