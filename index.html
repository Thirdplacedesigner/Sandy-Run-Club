Sandy Run Club

A School-Based Initiative for Health, Community, and Well-being

Discuss the data you collected/researched and identify the barriers your target group faces.

The primary barriers identified include a lack of personal time, low motivation, and social or body image concerns, including self-consciousness.

The primary social factor is how active the community one belongs to is. Teens are heavily influenced by how others act, feel or think. We, as the creator of a school-based community, can change these comprehensive attitudes toward physical activity inside the communities.

Explain how your initiative targets the barriers you identified. Include how this initiative will help your target group achieve their recommendations with the National Physical Activity Guidelines.

Our initiative is making Sandy Run Club, held from 7:30am to 8:40am on every Friday. The activities in this club are running from the school to the Sandringham beach, dipping in the water, and going to the café to relax, socialise, and get energy from a latte and friends.

By creating this energetic community, I can make the environment where teens have a positive mindset toward working out. Since negative self-consciousness has tremendous influence to prevent one from physical activity, the energetic atmosphere is pivotal in shaping positive exercising habits.

This run club is aiming for running for 30 minutes, which meets half the time of Moderate to Vigorous Physical Activity written in the National Physical Activity Guidelines. While it does not fully satisfy the criteria of 1-hour activity, completing a 30-minute activity before school is very effective and heightens the chance of meeting the goal.

Discuss the biggest barrier you faced or could face in the implementation of this initiative.

Although this run club does not have a minimal number of participants as shown in soccer or basketball clubs, gathering a decent number of students is required to make the atmosphere welcoming, warm, and energetic. It is going to be the biggest barrier for our initiative in the first phase.

Moreover, getting up early for running is hard for common teenagers who want to use social media, make breakfast, or sleep in the morning. Making the run club appealing for those individuals is challenging.

Identify a potential solution that would turn the barrier you identified into an enabler.

Participants of Sandy Run Club will get tickets for the canteen. They can present them at recess or lunchtime and get free snacks or food. By using a rewarding system, we can encourage people who don’t have a habit of working out in the morning to start a healthy habit.

The cost of giving tickets to participants will be supported by fundraising events regularly held inside the college.

Promotional techniques are used to improve physical activity levels. Explain the advantages and disadvantages of using a mass media technique like a poster.

Advantage: The primary advantage is that pictures or videos posted on the Sandy Run Club Instagram account can nudge many students to join this club. By gathering attention from many Instagram users besides students, this initiative works beyond Sandringham College and may encourage a huge number of teenagers worldwide to run with friends.

Disadvantage: The primary disadvantage is that the posts might stimulate self-consciousness and lead to a negative mindset arising from comparison with highly active people. Moreover, a lack of details in a video might provide misleading advice for teenagers and cause injuries.

The workplace is another example of ‘A settings-based approach’. Explain what ‘A Settings-based approach’ is and provide two reasons why the workplace is an effective place to implement an initiative.

A settings-based approach refers to a health promotion strategy that particularly applies to the characteristics of certain places, especially workplaces or residential buildings.

This method can be effectively applied to our initiative because building communities where people are active and support others’ workouts plays a crucial role in making a workout culture, as shown in the initial barriers. Moreover, once this community is built, others in a certain workplace (or school setting) can easily join and have fun with colleagues.
