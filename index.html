Begin The Adventure

Not a Morning Person? You Don't Have to Be an Athlete.

Forget intimidating fitness clubs and intense workouts. Sandy Run Club isn't about setting personal records—it's about starting your Friday morning with an unforgettable vibe.

If you've ever felt self-conscious, tired, or unmotivated in the morning, you belong here. Walk, jog, or chat at the back with friends. Every pace is celebrated.

"The hardest step is out your front door."

Feel the fresh coastal air, reset your mindset, and make memories before school even begins.

The Friday Routine

Every Friday from 7:30 AM to 8:40 AM. Three simple steps to kickstart your weekend early.

Step 01

The Beach Run

A fresh, 30-minute group run from school straight down to Sandringham Beach while the sun rises over the horizon.

Step 02

The Ocean Dip

Cool off, wash away the morning tiredness, and wake up your body with a refreshing splash in the cool ocean bay.

Step 03

Coffee & Hangout

Head over to the local café with the squad. Grab a warm latte or smoothie, recharge, and socialise before class starts.

Run Now. Eat Free Later.

Need an extra reason to jump out of bed? Show up for the morning run and earn Free Canteen Tickets for recess and lunch!

Free food, great friends, and half your recommended daily activity done before 9:00 AM.
