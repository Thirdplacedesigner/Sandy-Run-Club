Wake Up. Run. Caffeinate.

Not a Morning Person? We Get It.

Between homework, social media, and just needing some sleep, waking up early to exercise sounds like the last thing you want to do. Low motivation is real, and the thought of working out before school can be intimidating.

But what if mornings didn't have to be a drag? We built Sandy Run Club to change the way you start your day. It’s less about "working out" and more about starting a Friday adventure with your friends.

The Friday Routine

Every Friday from **7:30 AM to 8:40 AM**, we trade the snooze button for the sunrise. Here is the game plan:

  • The Run: A casual, 30-minute jog from the school straight down to Sandringham beach.
  • The Dip: Cool off and wake up with a quick splash in the water.
  • The Reward: Hit up the local café to grab a latte, socialize, and get energized before the first bell even rings.

For Everyone. Seriously.

You don't need to be a track star or a hardcore athlete to join us. If you've ever felt self-conscious about exercising or worried about keeping up, leave that at the door.

Sandy Run Club is about the community vibe. Whether you are jogging at the front or taking it easy at the back with a friend, you are part of the crew. We are here to build a positive, energetic environment where everyone belongs.

Run Now, Eat Free Later

Still need a little push to get out of bed? We've got you covered.

Every time you show up for a morning run, you earn **exclusive canteen tickets**. Present them during recess or lunchtime and treat yourself to free snacks and food. Build a healthy habit in the morning, get rewarded in the afternoon!

Check the Vibe

Don't just take our word for it—see it for yourself. Follow the Sandy Run Club Instagram account to catch the sunrise pics, beach dips, and cafe hangouts.

We are building a culture that goes beyond just Sandringham College. Join the movement, tag your friends, and let’s make Friday mornings legendary.

Your Journey Starts This Friday

Why wait? You are already knocking out half your recommended daily activity before school even starts, leaving your weekend completely free.

Grab your running shoes, bring a mate, and meet us this Friday. Let’s hit the beach and start the day right!
