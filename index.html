Not a Morning Person? We Get It.

Between homework, social media, and just needing some sleep, waking up early to exercise sounds intimidating.

But what if mornings didn't have to be a drag? We built Sandy Run Club to change the way you start your day. It’s less about "working out" and more about starting a Friday adventure with your friends.

You don't need to be a track star. If you've ever felt self-conscious about exercising, leave that at the door. Whether you are jogging at the front or walking at the back with a friend, you belong here.

"The hardest part is tying your shoes."

We are building a culture that goes beyond Sandringham College. Check our Instagram, catch the sunrise pics, and see the vibe for yourself.

The Friday Routine

Every Friday from 7:30 AM to 8:40 AM. Here is the game plan.

1. The Run

A casual, 30-minute jog from the school straight down the pavement to the beautiful Sandringham beach.

2. The Dip

Cool off, wash away the sleep, and completely wake up your body with a quick splash in the ocean water.

3. The Reward

Hit up the local café to grab a latte, socialize, and get energized before the first bell even rings.

Run Now, Eat Free Later.

Still need a little push to get out of bed?

Every time you show up for a morning run, you earn exclusive canteen tickets. Present them during recess or lunchtime and treat yourself to free snacks and food.

Build a healthy habit in the morning, get rewarded in the afternoon. It's a win-win.
